# UX review packet: PBI-06b · closing CTA spacing

## Context (fixed)
Closing section of a law firm's lead-generation landing pages: headline, one short paragraph, a red "Get a free case evaluation"
button, "or call (number)". Two pages had a different gap between the paragraph and the button; the operator chose the roomier one for all pages.

## Screenshots (desktop 1440 px)
1. `pbi06b-before-stop-calls`: before, page A (small gap), bottom of page incl. footer.
2. `pbi06b-before-lawsuit-attorney`: before, page B (roomy gap).
3. `pbi06b-after-stop-calls`: after, page A (closing section only).
4. `pbi06b-after-lawsuit-attorney`: after, page B (closing section only).

## Conclusions to challenge
1. After the change both pages show the same gap above the button.
2. The gap reads as intentional and balanced: the button is clearly grouped with its paragraph and the section still reads top to bottom.
3. Nothing else in the section moved or changed.
