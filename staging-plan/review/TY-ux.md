# UX review packet: thank-you page · first-screen actions and wording

## Context (fixed)
- A law firm's lead-generation landing pages (consumer-debt defense) on a staging copy. After a visitor submits the
  free case-evaluation form they land on this thank-you page. Its job: confirm the request, set expectations, and offer an
  immediate next step (call now or book a consultation).
- The page is shown in the firm's new design (white/soft-grey, near-black text, one red for actions).

## The change (after an earlier UX review)
1. "Call now" and "Schedule a consultation" moved from a section further down onto the first screen, under the confirmation.
2. Headline changed from "You have been pre-approved for a free legal consultation call with our attorney!" to
   "Thank you. Your request has been received." (the old wording was flagged as possibly over-assuring for a law firm).
   The new wording is marked "Wording pending legal review" on the page until an attorney approves it.

## Screenshots
1. `thanks-before-desktop`: the page as it is on staging today (old purple design).
2. `thanks-after-desktop`: proposed, desktop 1440×900 first screen.
3. `thanks-after-mobile`: proposed, phone 390×844 first screen.

## Conclusions to challenge
1. On both desktop and phone, a visitor can call or book without scrolling.
2. The call action is the most prominent action; booking is clearly second.
3. The new headline confirms the request without promising an outcome.
4. Nothing on the first screen competes with or covers the two actions.
