# UX review packet: design-system preview · one design for all 57 staging pages

## Context (fixed)
- A law firm's lead-generation landing pages (consumer-debt defense) on a Webflow staging copy (staging.credolegal.com).
  The pages' goal: the visitor starts the free case-evaluation form or calls.
- Today the 57 published pages come in three designs. 31 already use the new landing-page design (the reference):
  white/soft-grey sections, near-black ink text, one red (#c92028) for actions and accents, Hanken Grotesk for text and
  Inconsolata for labels, a 3-step form card. 21 pages still use an older purple design (#1c0326 hero, lavender form card,
  a brighter red, an older multi-step form). 5 are thank-you and system pages (two still purple/green, 404 and 401 are
  Webflow's unstyled defaults).
- This is a **proposal**, rendered as prototypes: nothing has changed on staging yet. Copy on the proposed pages is taken
  from each live page wherever it exists.

## Conclusions to challenge
1. Every proposed page reads as the same site as the reference landing page (colours, type, header, form, buttons).
2. On the state pages the state is still visible on the first screen.
3. On every proposed page the primary action (start the form, or call) is the most prominent element on the first screen.
4. The proposed 404 and 401 pages give a visitor a way back into the funnel.
5. Text on the proposed pages is at least as readable (size, contrast) as today.
