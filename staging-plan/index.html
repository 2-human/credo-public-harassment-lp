Why one row at a time

The board follows a Scrum WIP limit of one: only one row may have tasks in Doing. The whole team works on that one PBI together, a practice sometimes called swarming, and it is finished (published to staging and verified) before the next row starts.

Rows waiting on a decision keep their place in the priority order. When the decision arrives they are pulled in next; until then the next unblocked row is taken.
