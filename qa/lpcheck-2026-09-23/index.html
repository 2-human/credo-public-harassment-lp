G19 · On mobile, the sticky call bar covers the Continue button

Priority High · Status 24 Sep Open · phones only, during business hours

Cross-browser check (24 Sep). The generic "buttons covered" test in the cross-browser check did not flag this; the dedicated test described here did.

Problem. The phone-swap Embed script creates the fixed bar .sticky-call-button ("Is your debt situation becoming urgent? Call Now", 72px tall, z-index 9999999) only when the screen is ≤768px wide and it is business hours (Mon–Fri 9 AM–9 PM, Sat 9 AM–5 PM ET). What it covers, tested with the clock set inside business hours:

  • Step 1, first screen: where Continue lands depends on how many lines each page's H1 and sub-headline wrap to. Across all 30 pages, the bar covers Continue on first view on 21 pages: 19 on iPhone 15 (393×852) and/or 390×844, and 2 on Pixel 7 (412×915). On the other 9 pages it clears the button at every tested size. On iPhone SE (375×667) and Galaxy S8 (360×740), Continue always sits below the first screen. Each page section below says whether the bar covers Continue on that page.
  • Steps 2–3 (the pop-up form): the bar also sits on top of the pop-up (z-index 10000). The pop-up has 150px of bottom padding, so its buttons can be scrolled clear (steps 2 and 3 were walked on all 30 pages at iPhone 15 size, and both buttons were tappable at the bottom). The bar still covers fields while the visitor scrolls through them.
  • Desktop, laptop and tablets wider than 768px: no bar (it isn't created, and CSS hides it above 768px). At 768×1024 it sits well below Continue. Outside business hours there's no bar at any width.

The rendered prototype has no sticky bar (its stylesheet has a .sticky-cta rule, but this page variant never adds one to the page). The bar is worth keeping: in GA4 it's the most-clicked call element (17 clicks on enroll since 17 Sep against 3 and 2 for the other call links, and 54 on start).

Recommended solution in Webflow. Keep the bar, but show it only when no form is on screen: while the step-1 form card is in view it is hidden; once the visitor scrolls past the form it appears; while the pop-up form (steps 2–3) is open it is hidden; when the pop-up closes it returns.

  1. Starting state. The script creates the bar, so style it in the same Embed's <style>: add opacity:0; pointer-events:none; transition:opacity .2s; to .sticky-call-button, plus a rule .sticky-call-button.is-visible { opacity:1; pointer-events:auto; }.
  2. Show and hide on scroll. Webflow interactions can't target the bar, because it doesn't exist in the Designer; the script creates it after the page loads. So add a few lines to the same Embed script, right after the bar is appended: an IntersectionObserver that watches the step-1 form card (the wrapper around #gtmform) and toggles is-visible on the bar (add it when the card leaves the screen, remove it when the card is back). Watch the form card, not the whole hero section: on phones the hero also contains the photo below the form, so the bar would stay hidden longer than needed.
  3. Hide during the pop-up. The form script already adds the class mj-popup-open to the page body while the pop-up is open. Add one CSS line in Site settings → Custom code → Head code: body.mj-popup-open .sticky-call-button { display: none !important; }.
  4. Check on a 390×844 phone during business hours: first screen, Continue free and no bar; after scrolling past the form, the bar appears; tap Continue, the pop-up opens and the bar disappears; close the pop-up, the bar returns.
